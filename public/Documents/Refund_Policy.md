# Refund Policy

Thank you for choosing Meridiano Ceylon for your Sri Lankan travel experience. We value your trust and strive to provide a seamless booking process. If you need to cancel or modify your journey, we are here to help.

## 1. Booking Cancellations

All cancellation requests must be submitted in writing via email. As a travel provider, we incur operational costs with local hotels and transport partners immediately upon your booking. Therefore, the following cancellation charges apply:

- **More than 14 days before the tour date:** Full refund (excluding bank processing and transaction fees).
- **Between 7 and 14 days before the tour date:** 50% cancellation fee of the total booking value.
- **Less than 7 days before the tour date / no-show:** 100% cancellation fee; no refund will be issued.

## 2. The "Safety Guarantee" (Group Tours)

For our "Join a Group" Flash Deals, we offer a unique Safety Guarantee. If the minimum group size of 6 travelers is not met by the specified payment deadline (e.g., October 20th for November tours), you are eligible for a 100% refund. This refund includes your initial registration fee (e.g., $20 or $30) and any balance payments made.

## 3. Non-Refundable Items

Certain costs incurred by Meridiano Ceylon on your behalf are non-refundable once processed. These include:

- **Entrance tickets:** Fees for National Parks, Sigiriya, or other attractions are non-refundable once purchased.
- **Specialized permits:** Legal paperwork and permits for activities like Wildlife Photography Tours (which require 30 days' notice).
- **Visa fees:** Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) fees paid to the Department of Immigration and Emigration are subject to a strict no-refund policy.

## 4. Refund Processing Time

Once a refund is approved, it is processed through our payment partner, PayHere. The timeline for the funds to reach your account depends on the transaction type:

- **Instant refunds (same day):** If a refund is initiated on the same day as the payment, it is processed as a "Void." The amount is typically reflected in your account immediately or within 24 hours.
- **Delayed refunds (after 2 days):** If the refund is initiated after the payment has been settled to our bank (usually after 2 days), it can take 5 to 10 business days to reflect in your card statement.
- **Non-card payments:** Refunds for payments made via mobile wallets (e.g., mCash, eZcash) or direct bank transfers must be handled manually and may take 10 to 14 business days.

## 5. Weather and Force Majeure

Tours will not be refunded due to unfavorable weather unless an official closure is mandated by Sri Lankan authorities. In cases of government-mandated restrictions or natural disasters, we will prioritize rescheduling your tour or providing a credit voucher for future travel.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our refund policy or wish to initiate a cancellation, please contact our support team. We provide 24/7 assistance to ensure your experience with Meridiano Ceylon is hassle-free.
